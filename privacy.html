Privacy Policy

AtomMerge · Last updated 16 September 2026

This policy explains what happens to data when you play AtomMerge ("the Game"), a mobile game published by Mustafa Emir ("we", "us").

The short version. We do not collect, store or transmit any personal data ourselves. The Game has no account, no login and no server — your progress lives only on your own device. The only data that leaves your phone is what Google AdMob collects to show you ads.

1. Data we collect ourselves

None. The Game contains no analytics, no crash reporting and no networking code of its own. We never see your score, your progress or anything identifying you.

2. Data stored on your device

The Game saves your progress locally, using your device's standard app storage. This never leaves your phone and we cannot read it.

  • Current and best score
  • Coin balance
  • Daily missions, streak and gift status
  • Skins and merge effects you own and have equipped
  • Settings — language, music and sound volume, mute

Uninstalling the Game, or clearing its app data, deletes all of it permanently. There is no backup and no way for us to restore it.

3. Advertising

The Game shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure those ads, Google collects data directly from your device. We do not receive it, and we cannot access it — Google acts as an independent data controller for this processing.

What Google collectsWhy
Advertising ID (Android AAID)Ad targeting and frequency capping
IP addressApproximate location, fraud prevention
Device and OS informationAd compatibility and formatting
Ad interaction dataMeasuring views, clicks and rewards

Google's own handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Rewarded ads are optional. Ads that grant a reward — continuing a run, doubling the coins you earned — only play when you choose to watch one. You are never required to watch an ad to keep playing.

4. Your choices

  • Consent (EU/UK/Switzerland). On first launch the Game shows Google's consent form. You can decline personalised ads and still play; you will simply see less relevant ads. To change your choice later, open Options → Privacy Options.
  • United States residents. If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas or Virginia), you can opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of your personal information for targeted advertising at any time: open Options → Privacy Options in the Game. The Game keeps working the same way after you opt out.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID. Android: Settings → Google → Ads. iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Delete everything. Uninstall the Game, or clear its app data.

5. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any data from them. If you believe a child has provided data through the Game, contact us and we will help — though as described above, we hold no such data in the first place.

6. Purchases

The Game contains no real-money purchases. Every price in the shop is paid with coins earned by playing. We never handle payment information.

7. Data retention and transfer

We retain nothing, because we receive nothing. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained and transferred under Google's own policies, which may include processing outside your country.

8. Changes to this policy

If the Game starts collecting anything — analytics, cloud saves, leaderboards, in-app purchases — this page will be updated before that version ships, and the date at the top will change.
