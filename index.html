AtomMerge

A physics merge puzzle built on the periodic table.

Drop atoms into the container. Two of the same element touch, and they fuse into the next one up. Hydrogen becomes Helium, Helium becomes Lithium, and on up the table — as long as you can keep the stack below the danger line.

Features

  • 18 elements to climb, Hydrogen through Argon
  • Real 2D physics — every drop settles differently
  • Power-ups: bomb, ghost atom, magnet, freeze, upgrade, shake
  • Unlockable atom skins and merge effects, bought with coins you earn
  • Daily missions and a daily gift streak
  • English and Turkish
  • No real-money purchases

Privacy. AtomMerge has no account, no login and no server. Your progress is saved on your own device and never sent anywhere. The only data that leaves your phone is what Google AdMob collects to show ads. Read the full privacy policy.
